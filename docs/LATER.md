# LATER — Play Store launch, growth, and the parking lot

Not for v1. Kept here so ideas have a home and the scope stays tight.

## Play Store readiness (when JK is happy with v1)

- **Start recruiting testers early.** New personal Play Console accounts (created after 13 Nov 2023) must run
  a **closed test with at least 12 testers opted in for 14 consecutive days** before production access.
  Line up 15+ friends, family and designer contacts while v1 is still being polished.
- **Developer account:** $25 one-time, identity verification. Google's developer verification also applies to
  apps distributed outside Play from 2026–27; Play apps must be registered. A free "limited distribution"
  account allows sharing with up to 20 devices without Play.
- **Store assets:** final name, icon, 6–8 screenshots designed as a story, short video, feature graphic.
  Screenshots are the conversion lever — they're where the design advantage shows.
- **Policy:** privacy policy page (easy: no data collected), Data safety form (no data collected or shared),
  content rating, target audience.
- **Release:** Play App Signing, upload key = the release keystore from SETUP §5, App Bundle (`.aab`),
  staged rollout.
- **Quality bar:** Android vitals (crash and ANR rates), large screens and foldables check, 200% font,
  minSdk decision (31 vs lower for reach).

## Monetization (decided principles)

Free core forever. Optional **Supporter** one-time unlock (≈ $4.99–$9.99) for cosmetic extras only
(themes, icon variants, extra widget styles, yearly poster) and a tip jar. Never a subscription; never move
an existing feature behind a paywall.

## Getting the word out (ideas to evaluate later)

- **Launch timing:** May, ahead of the June Push-Up Challenge; and January (new-year habits).
- **Design communities first:** JK's network, Dribbble/Behance case study, X/Threads/LinkedIn build-in-public
  posts showing the glass and motion. The design is the hook.
- **Reddit:** r/androidapps, r/bodyweightfitness, r/getdisciplined — read each subreddit's self-promotion
  rules first (several are strict). Lead with "no ads, no account, no subscription".
- **Hacker News "Show HN"** and Product Hunt.
- **Google Play editorial:** pitch as a design showcase (Play's editors feature well-crafted indie apps).
- **ASO keywords:** push up counter, push up tracker, daily push ups, 100 push ups a day, push up challenge.
- **Shareable month recap card** as built-in word of mouth.

## Parking lot (considered, not now)

| Idea | Why it waits |
| --- | --- |
| iOS app | After Android v1 feels right; architecture already supports it |
| Wear OS tile / complication | High delight, low effort — first post-v1 candidate |
| Quick Settings tile | Easy; after widgets prove the habit |
| Camera (pose) rep counting | Accuracy issues hurt every app that leads with it; push-ups are the hard case |
| Proximity-sensor counting | Device-dependent and easy to fool |
| Other exercises | Top request, but the main source of competitor bloat |
| Friends / shared challenges | Moderation and cheating problems; maybe private small groups later |
| Grease-the-groove mode | Frequent small sets with reminders; fits the model, revisit after v1 |
| Year poster export | Supporter extra |
